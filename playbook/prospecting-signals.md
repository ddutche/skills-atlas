# Prospecting Signals

The actionable playbook. Only promote an entry here once a `knowledge/` pattern has enough
independent support to be worth acting on directly. Newest first.

Entry format:

```
### {YYYY-MM-DD} {short title}
- **Signal:** what to notice (an industry trait, a company stage, a stated complaint)
- **Pain pattern:** what it usually means underneath
- **Qualifying question:** how to confirm it in a conversation
- **Pitch angle:** how to frame the solution once confirmed
- **Evidence:** how many times this has held up, and how (see outcomes-log.md)
```
