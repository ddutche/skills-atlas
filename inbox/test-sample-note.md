Notes from a mock discovery call, for testing the intel-hub pipeline only. Not real customer
data.

A mid-size regional restaurant group mentioned their month-end close regularly slips past the
10th because their AP team manually re-keys vendor invoices from three different POS/ERP
exports into their accounting system, and nobody catches duplicate entries until the close
review. When asked about it, the controller said "we basically find out we double-paid someone
a month later, when the vendor calls asking why we sent it twice."

Separately, a grocery chain prospect said their biggest objection to switching platforms was
fear of losing historical vendor pricing history during migration, not the price of the new
tool itself.
